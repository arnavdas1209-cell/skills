# Platform Specifications

Read this when generating platform materials. For each platform: supported family, format, length limit, language, tone, and image guidance. Adapt the core message to each; do not paste the same text everywhere. Carry the 3 to 5 bullet-point highlights into every platform's material, in whatever form fits (a listed set, a structured thread, or scannable lines). All content stays faithful to the paper (see the one rule in SKILL.md).

## Blog
- **Family:** image-based (may also be text-led).
- **Length:** roughly 400 to 800 words.
- **Language:** English by default (or the author's audience language).
- **Tone:** informative and narrative; explain the what, why, how, and so-what in plain language.
- **Highlights:** include the bullet-point highlights, e.g. as a short "key points" list.
- **Images:** the graphical abstract plus one or two supporting visuals, next to the text they illustrate.
- **End with:** the paper link (DOI) and author/lab credit.

## WeChat channel (public account article)
- **Family:** image-based, or text plus image.
- **Length:** Chinese, under 1500 words; short paragraphs for phone reading.
- **Language:** Chinese.
- **Tone:** engaging and clear for an educated general Chinese audience; explain jargon.
- **Highlights:** carry the bullet-point highlights (要点) into the piece.
- **Images:** cover image plus the graphical abstract and key figures inline (image-based); or one supporting image (text plus image).
- **End with:** the paper link, author/lab, and journal name.

## Xiaohongshu (RED)
- **Family:** image-based, or text plus image.
- **Length:** Chinese, under 450 words; catchy title up to about 20 characters; body in short lines.
- **Language:** Chinese.
- **Tone:** casual, first-person, relatable; light emoji are normal; scannable.
- **Highlights:** present the highlights as scannable bullet lines.
- **Hashtags:** 5 to 10 relevant topic tags.
- **Images:** cover plus several images (image-based); or one to a few images (text plus image).
- **End with:** where to read more; keep the science accurate despite the casual register.

## LinkedIn
- **Family:** text plus image.
- **Length:** roughly 150 to 300 words; front-load the hook (first two lines show before "see more").
- **Language:** English by default.
- **Tone:** professional, first-person; suitable to tag co-authors, collaborators, and the institution.
- **Highlights:** the bullet-point highlights work well as the body's core.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5.  **Image:** the graphical abstract or one clear figure.
- **End with:** the paper link.

## X
- **Family:** text plus image.
- **Length:** 280 characters per post; split across a numbered thread for more.
- **Language:** English by default.
- **Tone:** concise and punchy; first post is a strong hook; 1 to 2 hashtags; image on the first post.
- **Highlights:** structure the thread around the highlights, one per post where it helps.
- **End with:** a final post with the DOI link and a brief call to read.

## Bluesky
- **Family:** text plus image.
- **Length:** 300 characters per post; single post or short thread.
- **Language:** English by default.
- **Tone:** concise, conversational; tags are fine but less algorithm-driven than X; include the link in the post.
- **Highlights:** fold the highlights in briefly; use a short thread if they do not fit one post.

## Cross-platform notes

- The plain-language summary is the faithful base; the bullet-point highlights are the reusable spine; each platform post is an adaptation of them, not a new set of claims.
- Every platform's material carries the highlights in a form that fits its format and limit.
- Reuse the graphical abstract as the anchor image across platforms where it fits.
- Keep the same honest scope everywhere: a short post still must not overclaim.
- Preserve the paper's scope qualifier (e.g. "in mice," "in vitro," "in a small cohort") and causal register (an association is "linked to," never "causes"/"cures," unless the paper itself uses causal language for that outcome) in the single highest-visibility unit of every post — the hook line, the title, or the first one to two lines — never only in a later, less-read line. Under a platform's length limit, shorten other words first; do not drop the hedge to make room.
- Always include the paper link, and credit co-authors and funders as appropriate.
